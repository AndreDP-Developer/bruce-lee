First, collect every lantern in the three courtyards. Return to the middle courtyard and drop through the newly opened hatch. Later rooms have their own gates and traps. The final wizard is defeated by reaching the switch.

Five lives. Original gameplay and room logic. Keyboard, touch and standard gamepad supported (A jumps, B or X fights). No second player.
